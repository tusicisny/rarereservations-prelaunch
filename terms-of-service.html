3. What the Service is, and what it isn't

3.1 What we do

We monitor the public availability of restaurants that use online booking platforms such as SevenRooms ("Booking Platforms"). We also learn when restaurants release new tables. When a table appears that matches one of your watches, we send you an alert with a link to the restaurant's own booking page, with your date and party size filled in.

In these Terms:

  • A watch is one request: one restaurant, one party size, a set of dates and an acceptable time window.
  • A Matching Table is a bookable time we detect that falls on one of a watch's dates and inside its time window, for its party size.
  • An alert is the message we send about a Matching Table by email, push notification, WhatsApp or text message (SMS), or any channel we add later.

3.2 What we don't do

  • We don't book for you. You book directly with the restaurant on the Booking Platform, under that platform's and that restaurant's terms. Deposits, prepayments, cancellation fees, no-show fees, dress codes and age rules are between you and the restaurant.
  • We don't log in to Booking Platforms or hold your platform credentials. We never ask for them.
  • We're independent. We aren't affiliated with, endorsed by or paid by any restaurant or Booking Platform. Restaurant and platform names are used only to identify them.
  • We don't guarantee a table. Popular tables can be gone within seconds, sometimes before an alert reaches you or before you finish booking. Restaurants and Booking Platforms can change availability, rules or pages at any time, show some tables only to certain customers, or stop us from reading their availability.
  • We don't guarantee delivery times. Speed estimates such as "usually within seconds" describe typical past performance, not a promise. Email providers, mobile carriers, WhatsApp, browser push services and your device affect delivery.

3.3 Information on our pages

Restaurant pages show release rules, cancellation policies, typical prices, opening hours, awards and similar facts. We source and date them, and some come from our own monitoring ("proprietary data"). They're our best information at the date shown, not a statement by the restaurant.
