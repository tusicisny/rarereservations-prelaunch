4. How we use your data

  1. To provide the Service: create and run your watches, find matching tables, send alerts on your channels, show your dashboard, and ask afterwards whether you got the table.
  2. To run your account: sign-in, sessions on your devices, settings, plan limits, and support.
  3. To take payments and meet tax rules: process payments, issue receipts and invoices, handle refunds, the Pass Guarantee and withdrawals, and keep tax records.
  4. To send service and account messages: sign-in and verification codes, receipts, payment problems, renewal reminders, security notices, and changes to our terms.
  5. To send useful tips about the Service you use, such as a release checklist before tables open, or how to set up push on iPhone. You can turn these off in Settings or with the link in each email.
  6. To send offers and updates, only if you opted in. You can withdraw at any time.
  7. To keep the Service secure: prevent fraud and abuse, enforce limits, detect bots, and investigate problems.
  8. To understand and improve the Service: measure sign-up and checkout funnels, test prices and features, and measure how fast alerts arrive and how often they lead to a booking.
  9. To meet legal obligations, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

8. International transfers

We're based in Spain, but our main systems run on Amazon Web Services in the United States, close to the booking platforms we monitor. Several of our providers are US companies. So your personal data is transferred to, and stored in, the United States.

We protect those transfers by:

  • relying on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, for providers certified under them
  • signing the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with our providers, with the UK addendum and Swiss amendments, so transfers stay protected even if the Framework is withdrawn
  • assessing the risks of the transfers, and using encryption in transit and at rest

You can ask for a copy of the safeguards we use at privacy@rarereservations.com.

10. Security

We protect your data with measures that include:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS only, with HSTS) and at rest
  • no passwords to steal; session tokens and verification codes are stored only as hashes
  • a database that isn't reachable from the internet
  • least-privilege access for our systems
  • verified signatures on webhooks from our providers
  • secrets kept in a secure store, never in code
  • security scans before every release

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we'll notify the authority and, where required, you.

11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • ask us to correct it
  • ask us to delete it

11.2 EEA, UK and Switzerland

You also have the right to:

  • restrict processing in some cases
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, including to useful tips; we'll stop unless we have compelling grounds
  • portability: receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another company
  • complain to a supervisory authority. Our lead authority is the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es). You can also complain where you live or work: in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), and in Switzerland to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch). We'd appreciate the chance to deal with your concern first.

11.3 How to make a request

Email privacy@rarereservations.com or support@rarereservations.com from your account email. For example, to delete your account, write "Delete my account" from the address you sign in with. If you write from another address, we'll ask you to confirm by replying from your account email, so no one else can get your data. You can use an authorized agent where your law allows; we'll ask for proof of their authority. We reply within 1 month. We may extend that by 2 more months for complex requests, and if so we'll tell you why. Requests are free unless they're clearly unfounded or excessive.
